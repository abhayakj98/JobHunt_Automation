# Resume Tailoring Guide

## Core Resume Architecture

- Maintain one global base resume in Markdown.
- Treat every application resume as a role-specific derivative.
- Keep Hindustan Unilever grouped under one employer section.
- Keep Nestle R&D merged when continuity helps.
- Use no more than 4 skill buckets.

## Non-Negotiable Structure Rules

- Contact block
- Summary
- Current experience
- Past experience
- Relevant skills
- Education
- Awards / leadership

If a section must shrink for space, trim low-signal content first.

## Summary Rules

- The first sentence must describe the candidate in the language of the target role.
- Keep `data-oriented, insight-driven marketing professional` as a reusable anchor when it fits.
- Use `6 years` or `6+ years` only when the framing remains defensible for the target role.
- Do not carry brand-heavy language into analytics or storefront roles if it hurts fit.

## Role Family Mapping

### Brand / Category Marketing

- Lead with premium positioning, consumer insight, partnerships, media, storytelling, and brand growth
- Foreground:
  - Lakme Fashion Week repositioning
  - media planning
  - integrated campaigns
  - awareness / penetration / premiumisation

### Product Marketing / GTM

- Lead with GTM strategy, growth experiments, launches, digital asset optimization, cross-functional execution, analytics
- Foreground:
  - A/B testing
  - growth playbooks
  - platform strategy
  - launch execution
  - AI-tool usage when truthful

### Storefront / Growth Marketing

- Lead with trials, retention, lifetime value, customer engagement, conversion, operating rigor
- Foreground:
  - digital optimization
  - experimentation
  - performance marketing
  - data quality
  - actionability

### Analytics / Measurement Marketing

- Lead with research, analysis, measurement, experimentation, reporting, and marketing recommendations
- Foreground:
  - A/B testing
  - consumer-journey analysis
  - MMM
  - reporting routines
  - decision support
- Never force unsupported keywords like `SQL`, `LTV`, or `churn prediction`

## Evidence Selection Rules

- Pull bullets upward when they directly mirror the JD
- Rephrase operational work into customer, conversion, or decision-making impact where truthful
- De-emphasize backend analytics if it does not clearly support business outcomes

## Titles, Subheads, And Impact Bars

- Preserve the original role title unless changing it materially improves candidacy
- Make subheads metric-led and scan-friendly
- Good pattern:
  - `Go-To-Market, Growth Marketing & Brand Strategy | 1200 bps Penetration Gain | 100 Cr Media Strategy`

## Content Prioritization

- Prefer recent, job-relevant awards
- Add `Best Experiential Marketing Activation in Homecare` when it strengthens fit
- Remove school scores or sports content before cutting stronger role evidence

## Line Tightening Rules

- If a line spills by 1-2 words, shorten the line before shrinking fonts
- Remove low-signal connectors or redundant qualifiers first
- Keep substance intact

Examples:
- `using consumer journey insights to sharpen channel prioritization and growth investments`
  → `using consumer journey insights to sharpen channel priorities and growth investments`
- `owning 100 Cr media investment strategy`
  → `100 Cr Media Strategy`

## Honesty Rules

- Never add tools, category depth, or experience that the source material does not support
- If a user asks for harmful wording, note the conflict and optimize for a submit-worthy resume instead
