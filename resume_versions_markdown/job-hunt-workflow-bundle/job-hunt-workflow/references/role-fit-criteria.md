# Role Fit Criteria

## Active Brief Handling

- Reconstruct the latest locked brief from project history before starting new sourcing.
- Treat the brief as an operating constraint, not a loose preference.
- Refresh only when the user explicitly changes seniority, geography, sectors, or role families.

## Current High-Signal Candidate Persona

- 6 years across Hindustan Unilever, Bain, and Nestle
- Strongest evidence: consumer insight, GTM, brand building, experimentation, e-commerce / q-commerce, analytics-led execution, marketing campaign, integrated marketing, media planning, impact measurement, proposition, positioning, performance marketing
- Best-fit role families:
  - brand / category marketing
  - product marketing / GTM
  - storefront / growth marketing
  - analytics-led marketing roles

## Geography And Seniority Rules

- India: open across cities unless the brief says otherwise
- Outside India: include only exceptional-fit roles
- Explicitly call out when a role is a stretch on seniority, category depth, or geography

## Screening Workflow

1. Check whether the role is already in the approved sheet, local `jobs_markdown/`, or prior shortlist outputs.
2. Validate current status and recency.
3. Screen for:
   - role family alignment
   - minimum years match
   - industry / category fit
   - geography fit
   - realism of transition from current profile
4. Reject or demote if the role is closed, stale, duplicate, too senior, or outside the brief.

## Maker-Checker Pattern

### Maker

- Finds candidate roles
- Drafts the shortlist
- Provides first-pass fit rationale

### Checker

- Verifies status, fit, and seniority
- Demotes roles that are attractive but inefficient
- Flags overreach, duplicates, and weak category transfer

## Fit Scoring Rubric

### Strong Fit

- Role family aligns directly
- Experience bar is met or close enough to defend
- Sector and required proof overlap strongly
- Resume can be tailored without distortion

### Moderate Fit

- One gap exists, but the role is still worth considering
- Usually a category-transfer or seniority stretch

### Low ROI

- Multiple gaps, weak transferability, or an obvious screening blocker

## Source Preferences

- Prefer official career pages
- Use LinkedIn, Wellfound, Foundit, Naukri, and mirrors as secondary discovery layers
- If extraction is blocked, document fallback assumptions in the JD markdown

## Output Standard

Return only:
- prioritized shortlist
- links
- fit rationale
- explicit caveats

Do not recommend a role casually if it fails the brief.
