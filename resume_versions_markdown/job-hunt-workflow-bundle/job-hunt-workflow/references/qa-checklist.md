# QA Checklist

## Phase 1: Brief And Fit Check

- Is the role screened against the active brief?
- Is the role family correctly identified?
- Are any stretch conditions explicitly called out?

## Phase 2: JD Markdown Check

- Does the JD file have the required frontmatter?
- Does it include all standard sections?
- Is source quality truthful?
- Are fallback assumptions documented?

## Phase 3: Resume Content Check

- Does the summary speak in the language of the target role?
- Is HUL grouped correctly?
- Is Nestle structured correctly when included?
- Are there 4 skill buckets or fewer?
- Are all user non-negotiables covered unless they clearly hurt role fit?
- Are unsupported ATS terms excluded?

## Phase 4: Coherence Check

- Any duplicated headers?
- Any conflicting seniority framing?
- Any mixed narrative across brand, PMM, and analytics styles?
- Does the resume remain defensible in interview?

## Phase 5: Render And Spillover Check

- Does the HTML render cleanly?
- Are any bullets spilling by 1-2 words?
- Have those lines been tightened before touching layout?
- Does the page feel balanced rather than compressed?

## Phase 6: Deliverable Check

- Markdown exists
- HTML exists
- PDF exists
- Output naming matches conventions
- PDF is one page

## Final Acceptance Standard

The resume should:
- scan clearly in under 30 seconds
- reflect the target role family
- preserve truthful evidence
- be ready to submit without caveats
