# Music Business Manager, India

## Snapshot
- Company: Apple
- Team: Marketing / Music business
- Location: Mumbai
- Minimum experience: 8 years

## Role Summary
This role is about growing Apple's music business in India through strategy, partnerships, market insight, and cross-functional execution. It reads more like a market-development and ecosystem-growth role than a classic consumer brand-marketing position.

## Core Responsibilities
- Help define the India music-business strategy with local and global leaders.
- Own partnerships across labels, platforms, tech partners, and brands.
- Work with business development teams to expand reach and engagement.
- Track performance, resolve partner issues, and improve execution.
- Use market and competitive insight to spot growth opportunities.
- Coordinate with editorial, operations, marketing, and other internal teams.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 8+ years of total experience.
- Strong understanding of streaming, the music industry, and market dynamics.
- Experience with global teams and external ecosystem partners.
- Strong analytics, senior-stakeholder communication, and execution discipline.
- MBA or Master's preferred; English plus at least one Indian language required.
