# Product Marketing Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Paytm
- Focus area: Paytm Ads
- Locations: Bangalore, Mumbai, Noida
- Minimum experience: 2 years

## Role Summary
This is a product-marketing role for Paytm Ads. The emphasis is on messaging, sales enablement, content strategy, and advertiser engagement rather than pure consumer brand marketing.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own the content and communication strategy for Paytm Ads.
- Create sales collateral such as decks, case studies, playbooks, and explainers.
- Improve visibility with advertisers and agencies.
- Run events, webinars, and partner-facing engagement programs.
- Support launches and feature updates with targeted marketing.
- Work closely with sales and business teams to convert product capability into clear narratives.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 2-3 years in product marketing or B2B marketing.
- Strong storytelling and content development.
- Ability to simplify complex product capabilities.
- Comfortable in a fast-moving environment.
