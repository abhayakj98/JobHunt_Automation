# Senior Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Cultfit
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 7 years
- Fit: consumer brand leadership in fitness, wellness, FMCG, apparel, or adjacent lifestyle categories

## Role Summary
This role owns brand strategy across Cultfit's fitness and product verticals. The brief centers on long-term brand building, sharper narrative development, brand health measurement, media mix decisions, and high-visibility activations that make the brand more culturally relevant and commercially effective.

## Core Responsibilities
- Build long-range brand strategy aligned with Cultfit's goal of expanding fitness adoption.
- Turn consumer insight into campaign narratives and growth ideas.
- Improve brand measurement and use those signals in decision-making.
- Guide briefing quality, message hierarchy, and creative collaboration.
- Manage media strategy across traditional and digital channels.
- Run events, partnerships, and activations that strengthen community positioning.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 7 to 9 years of experience in brand management.
- Proven ownership in a consumer lifestyle category.
- Strong campaign execution, media planning, and brand tracking skills.
- Strong storytelling, communication, and stakeholder management.
- Analytical, data-backed decision making.
