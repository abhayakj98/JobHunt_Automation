# Sr. Storefront Marketing Manager, Prime Video IN, Prime Video IN

## Snapshot
- Company: Amazon
- Location: Mumbai
- Minimum experience: 6 years
- Team context: Prime Video storefront growth across ANZ, sports content, and Southeast Asia market segments

## Role Summary
This is an individual-contributor storefront-growth role for Prime Video. The work is built around customer engagement metrics, experimentation, x-site merchandising, personalization, and regional operating plans that improve visibility and conversion across multiple markets.

## Core Responsibilities
- Drive and support customer engagement metrics across multiple regions and market segments.
- Audit and update data pipelines with Business Intelligence and Tech teams to improve data quality.
- Define and run tests, including A/B experiments, to evaluate features and inform product development.
- Drive strategic placement and optimization of Prime Video content across Amazon retail websites.
- Execute customer-driven storefront strategies using personalization, global tools, and manual overrides.
- Work closely with global Storefront Product teams and regional stakeholders to optimize performance.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 6+ years of professional non-internship marketing experience.
- Experience using data and metrics to drive improvements.
- Experience with Excel or Tableau, including data manipulation and reporting.
- Experience building, executing, and scaling cross-functional marketing programs.
- Experience communicating results to senior leadership.
- Experience with A/B testing or multivariate testing.
