# Senior Manager - Brand (Faces Canada)

## Snapshot
- Company: Purplle.com
- Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
- Status: open/live
- Minimum experience: 5+ years post-MBA
- Core theme: omnichannel beauty brand ownership, launches, and P&L growth

## Role Summary
This role owns the Faces Canada brand and drives end-to-end business performance across product launches, consumer insight, pricing, packaging, communication, and go-to-market strategy. It is a strong omnichannel beauty and lifestyle role with both brand and commercial accountability.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own overall brand performance and P&L.
- Lead the full product launch lifecycle from trendspotting and insights through pricing, packaging, communication, and GTM.
- Collaborate with marketing, R&D, supply chain, and sales to scale launches and sustained growth.
- Partner with online and offline channel teams to shape portfolio and category performance across platforms.
- Drive brand growth in a fast-moving omnichannel beauty environment.

## Requirements Snapshot
- MBA from a reputed B-school.
- 5+ years of post-MBA work experience.
- Experience with consumer brands known for innovation, strong NPD, and rapid growth.
- Prior experience in beauty, skincare, or related consumer categories preferred.
- Strong analytical skills, business acumen, and instinct for consumer trends and brand building.
