# Brand Manager Bacardi

## Snapshot
- Company: Bacardi
- Location: Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 6 years
- Brand context: premium consumer brand with strong music, culture, and community marketing orientation

## Role Summary
This role is centered on culture-led brand building for Bacardi. It emphasizes experiential activations, partnerships, integrated storytelling, and close coordination with commercial, customer marketing, media, PR, and social teams.

## Core Responsibilities
- Lead large-scale experiential activations and partnerships as the on-site brand lead.
- Own planning, execution, and post-campaign analysis of key brand programs.
- Align consumer programs with local commercial and customer-marketing teams.
- Build integrated earned, owned, and paid storytelling with media, PR, and social partners.
- Manage creative agencies, production partners, and execution workflows.
- Identify new music, culture, and community partnerships that extend brand relevance.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing or a related field; MBA preferred.
- 6-8 years in brand, experiential, or agency marketing.
- Experience in beverage, alcohol, lifestyle, or adjacent premium categories preferred.
- Proven large-scale activation and project-management experience.
- Strong relationship management and cross-functional collaboration.
- Experience across digital, social, experiential, media, and sponsorships.
