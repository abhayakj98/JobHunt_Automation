# Senior Product Marketing Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: SmartBear
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 7 years
- Product context: BugSnag / observability

## Role Summary
This is a technical B2B SaaS PMM role for SmartBear's BugSnag business. The job is focused on positioning, launches, demand collaboration, sales enablement, and pipeline impact for developer-facing observability products.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own end-to-end product-marketing strategy for BugSnag, including positioning and differentiation.
- Lead GTM planning for launches, feature releases, and growth initiatives.
- Partner with Product on roadmap priorities using research, insights, and competitive intelligence.
- Collaborate with Growth Marketing on demand programs and product-led growth initiatives.
- Create narratives and enable Sales and Sales Engineering with decks, demos, and battlecards.
- Drive qualified pipeline and adoption for developer-focused observability products.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 7+ years in product marketing, ideally in B2B SaaS developer tools, infrastructure, or observability.
- Strong technical acumen around observability, error monitoring, or application performance monitoring.
- Experience marketing to developers, SREs, platform engineers, and engineering leaders.
- Demonstrated pipeline impact through partnership with growth or demand-generation teams.
- Strong storytelling, communication, and cross-functional leadership.
