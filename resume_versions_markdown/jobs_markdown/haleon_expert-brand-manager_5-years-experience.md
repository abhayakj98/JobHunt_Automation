# Expert Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Haleon
- Location: Gurgaon, Haryana, India
- Status: open/live
- Minimum experience: 5+ years
- Core theme: consumer-health brand growth for the oral health portfolio

## Role Summary
This role is centered on building and executing brand growth plans for Haleon's oral health portfolio in India. It combines market analysis, brand communication, omni-channel activation, stakeholder engagement, and commercial performance tracking in a consumer-health setting.

## Core Responsibilities
- Lead annual market activation planning and brand strategy execution.
- Use market analysis and consumer or HCP insight to identify growth opportunities.
- Build omni-channel campaigns across in-clinic, mobile, email, events, and other touchpoints.
- Own marketing budgets, activation ROI, and performance reporting.
- Partner with sales, medical, agencies, and external stakeholders to strengthen brand communication and accessibility.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Master's degree.
- 5 or more years in consumer healthcare or pharma.
- At least 2+ years of marketing or commercial management experience.
- HCP product marketing experience is highly desirable.
- Comfort working in a fast-paced, performance-focused environment.
