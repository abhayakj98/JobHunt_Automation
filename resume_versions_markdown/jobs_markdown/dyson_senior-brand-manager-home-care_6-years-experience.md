# Senior Brand Manager - Home Care

## Snapshot
- Company: Dyson
- Location: Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 6 years
- Portfolio context: floor care, environmental care, lighting, and Airblade

## Role Summary
This is an integrated category-marketing role for Dyson's India home-care business. The role combines strategy, digital-first media planning, launch execution, category vision, and P&L-style commercial ownership.

## Core Responsibilities
- Devise category strategy from consumer insight, digital and e-commerce data, and retail understanding.
- Build and execute category plans to hit share, funnel, and commercial targets.
- Own category budget and annual planning.
- Develop communication strategy and execute a primarily digital media plan.
- Partner with PR and cross-functional teams to deliver launches and promotions.
- Shape the long-term category vision and roadmap with global commercial teams.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 6-8 years of marketing experience in FMCG or consumer technology.
- FMCG or FMCD background, ideally with category creation and scale-up exposure.
- MBA from a premier business school preferred.
- Strong execution, project-management, and multitasking ability.
- Strong analytical thinking plus Excel and PowerPoint fluency; engineering background is a plus.

- Headline angle: Insight-led category marketer who can scale premium consumer products through digital-first planning.
- Foreground evidence: launch planning, media strategy, consumer journey mapping, and commercial outcomes tied to market share or growth.
