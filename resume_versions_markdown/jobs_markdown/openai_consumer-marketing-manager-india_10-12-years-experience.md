# Consumer Marketing Manager, India

## Snapshot
- Company: OpenAI
- Location: India - Remote, with role flexibility across Delhi, Mumbai, or Bangalore
- Experience requirement: 10-12 years
- Core mandate: build and scale India-specific consumer marketing initiatives that drive adoption, trust, and brand growth

## Role Summary
This is a senior India consumer-marketing role for OpenAI. The remit is full-stack and locally grounded: shape India-specific strategy, drive integrated campaigns across online and offline channels, represent India’s voice internally, and work with local agencies and production partners to deliver culturally resonant programs.

## Core Responsibilities
- Lead India-specific marketing strategy aligned to global business goals.
- Drive full-funnel campaigns across online and offline channels.
- Bring local expertise on Indian culture, user behavior, and media landscapes into strategy and creative development.
- Manage local agencies, creatives, and production partners.
- Partner with product marketing, communications, partnerships, and regional leadership on integrated programs.
- Ensure Indian market context is reflected in messaging, execution, and campaign design.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 10-12 years of experience in marketing with strong India campaign delivery across multiple audience segments, including consumer.
- Deep understanding of Indian consumer behavior, cultural context, and regional media dynamics.
- Proven integrated marketing experience across brand, digital, influencer, and partner channels.
- Comfortable operating strategically and executionally in fast-moving environments.
- Strong local agency / creative / production-partner management experience.
- Strong communication and stakeholder-influence skills.

## Fit Assessment
- Strongest overlaps:
  - integrated ATL / BTL / digital / PR / social campaign experience
  - India consumer insight and regional market execution
  - agency and vendor management
  - measurable awareness, penetration, and growth outcomes
  - cross-functional leadership across media, content, sales, finance, and regional teams
- Main gap:
  - the JD explicitly asks for 10-12 years; this is a senior stretch relative to the current profile

## Resume Tailoring Signals
- Headline angle: India consumer marketer with integrated campaign execution, strong local insight, and measurable growth outcomes.
- Foreground evidence: Lakme Fashion Week launch, integrated media / PR / social campaigns, awareness lifts, agency management, regional market localization, and demand generation.
- Mirror these phrases naturally: consumer marketing, India-specific strategy, integrated campaigns, adoption, trust, online and offline channels, local insight, agencies, cross-functional programs.
- De-emphasize: storefront-only or heavily product-ops language that is not clearly tied to brand, consumer behavior, or integrated campaign execution.

## Application Positioning
- This should be framed as a high-upside stretch application, not a literal years-match application.
- The resume should optimize for credibility: strong India consumer-marketing proof, local resonance, integrated execution, and measurable outcomes without inflating seniority.
