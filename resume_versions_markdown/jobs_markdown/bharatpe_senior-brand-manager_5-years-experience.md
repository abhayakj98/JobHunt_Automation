# Senior Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: BharatPe
- Location: Gurugram
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Category context: consumer-fintech brand and GTM role

## Role Summary
This role is centered on building BharatPe's narrative through campaigns, GTM planning, partnerships, and coordinated execution with growth and creative teams. It blends brand development with business and performance orientation.

## Core Responsibilities
- Conceptualize marketing campaigns, engagement plans, and GTMs for products and features.
- Execute brand partnerships with KOLs and B2B collaborators.
- Align internal stakeholders with creative and brand leadership.
- Support growth and performance marketing to hit monthly business numbers.
- Craft and communicate the brand narrative across teams.
- Drive cross-functional rollout of brand and GTM initiatives.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 5-10 years in brand or integrated marketing.
- Strong campaign and GTM execution background.
- Experience with KOL and B2B partnerships.
- Comfort supporting performance marketing and business targets.
- Strong cross-functional communication and ownership.
