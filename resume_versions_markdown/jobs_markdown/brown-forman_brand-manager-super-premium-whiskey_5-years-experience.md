# Brand Manager - Super Premium+ Whiskey

## Snapshot
- Company: Brown-Forman
- Location: Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Portfolio cue: super-premium whiskey brands including Woodford Reserve and Glendronach

## Role Summary
This is a premium brand-building role for Brown-Forman's India whiskey portfolio. The job is built around 360 brand planning, innovation, consumer insight, collaborations, and premium growth rather than pure execution support.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own the 3-year brand-building roadmap and 1-year brand plan.
- Lead the full 360 consumer communication and engagement plan across social, experiential, PR, and collaborations.
- Own NPD and innovation planning and execution for the portfolio.
- Serve as the internal voice of the consumer and competitor landscape.
- Manage budget with a strong ROI focus and work with external partners and agencies.
- Build trade marketing campaigns using the Brown-Forman growth framework.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Brand marketing experience in super-premium or luxury consumer products, lifestyle brands, or alcobev.
- Strong ability to convert structured and unstructured consumer data into action.
- Hands-on experience with social and content campaigns plus brand collaborations.
- Strong budget discipline, ROI orientation, and cross-functional delivery.
- Comfort working independently in a fast-moving environment.
