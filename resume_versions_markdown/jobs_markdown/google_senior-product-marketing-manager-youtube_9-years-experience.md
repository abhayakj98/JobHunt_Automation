# Senior Product Marketing Manager, YouTube

## Snapshot
- Company: Google
- Location: Gurugram / Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 9 years
- Product context: YouTube creator ecosystem

## Role Summary
This is a senior PMM role focused on YouTube creator marketing and global go-to-market execution. The work is centered on product launches, creator ecosystem growth, prioritization, and alignment across complex cross-functional stakeholders.

## Core Responsibilities
- Lead YouTube creator marketing across complex product areas and ecosystem challenges.
- Provide marketing leadership to influence strategies that impact YouTube.
- Set creator-marketing strategy to support YouTube business goals such as monetization and satisfaction.
- Plan and execute GTM for priority creator-ecosystem launches, including awareness and adoption.
- Lead development and execution of a coherent global strategy during planning cycles.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 9 years of marketing experience across growth, PMM, brand marketing, or social.
- Experience managing cross-functional or cross-team projects.
- Ability to influence partners and align stakeholders around strategy and execution.
- Strong communication and organizational skills, including comfort with C-level stakeholders.
