# Product Marketing Manager, Google Search

## Snapshot
- Company: Google
- Location: Gurugram
- Minimum experience: 6 years
- Status note: official Google Careers listing is currently open

## Role Summary
This PMM role is focused on shaping how Google Search is marketed in India. The work combines local market strategy, go-to-market planning, measurement, and cross-functional launch execution as Search evolves with AI-led improvements.

## Core Responsibilities
- Drive GTM strategy, planning, and execution for Google Search in India.
- Partner with product teams to deliver insights, launch, and communicate Search features in India.
- Develop growth marketing strategies and an experiment plan.
- Conduct analysis to support marketing strategy, impact, and measurement.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams, external agencies, and external partners to execute plans.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 6 years of marketing experience across growth, PMM, brand marketing, or social.
- Experience managing cross-functional or cross-team projects.
- Experience with metrics, analytics, and using data to inform marketing or product strategy.
- Experience in developing and executing high-quality brand strategy and creative execution.
- Experience in developing growth marketing both in-app and off-app.
- Ability to use AI tools effectively, solve problems, and communicate with strong business judgment.
