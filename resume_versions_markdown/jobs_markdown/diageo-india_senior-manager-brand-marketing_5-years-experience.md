# Senior Manager - Brand Marketing

## Snapshot
- Company: DIAGEO India
- Location: Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 5 years directly on relevant lifestyle or culture-led brands
- Brand context: Johnnie Walker Red Label and Blonde

## Role Summary
This role leads brand marketing for a major DIAGEO portfolio with clear emphasis on culture-led growth. It blends strategic planning with integrated execution across media, partnerships, experiences, packaging architecture, and innovation.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own the brand growth agenda for Johnnie Walker Blonde.
- Lead marketing business planning and growth-driver development with ROI and learning plans.
- Execute across media, content, partnerships, experiences, and customer channels.
- Run brand performance reviews and influence internal business forums.
- Drive influencer and advocacy strategy with insights and analytics partners.
- Own brand, communication, and packaging architecture while partnering on innovation.

## Requirements Snapshot
- MBA in Marketing from a Tier I or Tier II institute.
- 8-10 years of progressive marketing experience.
- At least 5 years managing lifestyle, youth-led, or culture-driven brands.
- Experience with digital-first ecosystems, partnerships, and experiential platforms preferred.
- Strong consumer-insight, analytical, storytelling, and commercial skills.
