# Associate Director - Brand Marketing

## Snapshot
- Company: Noise
- Location: Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 8 years
- Scope: full brand charter across narrative, launches, social, PR, and partnerships

## Role Summary
This is a senior consumer-brand leadership role for Noise. The remit covers brand positioning, launch storytelling, social and influencer strategy, PR, and long-term brand architecture with strong emphasis on Indian consumer culture and measurable brand performance.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own and evolve Noise's brand identity, tone of voice, and narrative across platforms.
- Define long-term brand vision aligned to business goals and product roadmap.
- Lead overall social-media and content strategy across always-on and campaign work.
- Own PR, influencer, and brand partnerships to extend reach and credibility.
- Lead brand-led GTM for new launches and categories with Product, Growth, and Performance teams.
- Track and optimize brand and launch performance using data and consumer insights.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 8-12 years of brand-marketing experience, preferably with a postgraduate degree.
- Strong understanding of Indian consumer culture, digital ecosystems, and creator-led marketing.
- Proven record of building distinctive brands and executing high-impact launches.
- Experience managing large budgets, teams, and agency partners.
- Strong storytelling, stakeholder-management, and data-driven decision making.
