# Senior Marketing Analyst, Gemini Marketing

## Snapshot
- Company: Google
- Location: Gurugram, Haryana, India; Hyderabad, Telangana, India
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Status note: official Google Careers listing is currently open

## Role Summary
This role sits within Gemini Marketing and is focused on measurement, analytics, and marketing decision support for a fast-evolving AI product. The work emphasizes translating business problems into research questions, building campaign measurement frameworks, and using data to improve marketing effectiveness and product adoption.

## Core Responsibilities
- Design and deliver research and analysis projects that answer key Gemini marketing questions.
- Translate business problems into measurement frameworks, hypotheses, and marketing recommendations.
- Build measurement plans, metrics, benchmarks, and tracking requirements for campaigns.
- Analyze campaign performance and report effectiveness across stakeholder groups.
- Work with agencies and cross-functional teams to align on methodology, success metrics, and decision-making.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Bachelor's degree in a research or quantitative field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 5 years of experience designing, scoping, executing, and delivering research and analysis projects.
- 5 years of experience translating business problems into research questions and research findings into marketing recommendations.
- Experience managing research and measurement agencies.
- Preferred: experience measuring a fast-evolving consumer app or AI-driven product.
- Preferred: experience with data clustering, lifetime value modeling, churn prediction, SQL, or comparable analytical tools.
