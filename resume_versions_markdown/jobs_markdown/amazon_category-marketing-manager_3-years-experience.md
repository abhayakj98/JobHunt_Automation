# Category Marketing Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Amazon
- Team: Amazon Consumables
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 3 years

## Role Summary
The role sits in the India Consumables Marketing team and focuses on traffic growth, merchandising, cohort analysis, and demand generation for the category. It is a category-growth role with strong performance marketing, experimentation, and analytics components.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own key traffic drivers for the category and influence central traffic teams.
- Shape homepage and category page strategy.
- Drive growth through search, social, push, affiliates, email, and SEO.
- Analyze cohorts and run experiments across coupons, gamification, and influencer-led initiatives.
- Manage merchandising calendars, promotions, and funnel diagnostics.
- Measure impact and report insights to senior leadership.

## Requirements Snapshot
- 3+ years of marketing experience outside internships.
- Comfort using metrics and data to improve results.
- Experience building and scaling cross-functional marketing programs.
- Strong Excel skills; Tableau is also mentioned.
