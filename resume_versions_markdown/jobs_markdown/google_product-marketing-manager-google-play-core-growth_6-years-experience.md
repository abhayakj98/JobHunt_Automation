# Product Marketing Manager, Google Play Core Growth

## Snapshot
- Company: Google
- Location: Bengaluru
- Status: open/live
- Core theme: consumer growth and product marketing for Google Play

## Role Summary
This role sits at the intersection of product marketing and consumer growth for Google Play. The work emphasizes CRM, push and lifecycle marketing, identifying user-journey pain points, and running experiments that improve engagement and conversion.

## Core Responsibilities
- Shape go-to-market strategy for Google Play core growth initiatives.
- Partner cross-functionally to identify user-journey friction and growth opportunities.
- Build and execute CRM, push marketing, and lifecycle programs.
- Use experiments and analytics to test messaging, offers, and user journeys.
- Coordinate across product, marketing, and other teams to deliver launches and growth plans.

## Requirements Snapshot
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 6 years of experience in marketing across growth, product marketing, brand marketing, or social.
- Experience managing cross-functional or cross-team projects.
- Preferred: 8 years developing global marketing programs with global stakeholders.
- Preferred: experience with push marketing, notifications, CRM, gaming, mobile, loyalty, or e-commerce.
- Preferred: ability to identify user-journey pain points and solve problems with bias to action.
