# Senior Marketing Manager – Brand Equity (Online Ordering)

## Snapshot
- Company: Zomato
- Location: Gurugram
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Scope: Brand equity for Zomato's core online ordering business

## Role Summary
Drive brand equity initiatives for Zomato's online ordering business. Charter national growth plans and translate them into strong ATL/BTL marketing strategies. Partner with merchants for customised campaigns, track offline marketing ROI, and optimise spends across mediums. Mine consumer insights with analytics teams to develop actionable marketing plans.

## Core Responsibilities
- Build and execute national brand equity campaigns for online ordering.
- Develop ATL/BTL strategy and translate plans into execution.
- Work with analytics teams to mine consumer insights.
- Optimise marketing mix and measure ROI across channels.
- Partner with merchant teams for co-marketing campaigns.
- Track brand metrics and course-correct based on performance data.

## Key Requirements
- 5-6 years in brand management / national brand marketing
- Marketing mix management, ATL/BTL planning
- Strong analytical skills and business acumen
- Consumer insights and research experience
- Comfort with digital and performance marketing
