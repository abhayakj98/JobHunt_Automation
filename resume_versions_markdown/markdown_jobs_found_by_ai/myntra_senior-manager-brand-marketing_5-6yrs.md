# Senior Manager – Brand Marketing

## Snapshot
- Company: Myntra (Flipkart Group)
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Scope: Fashion and lifestyle brand equity

## Role Summary
Lead brand equity initiatives for Myntra's fashion and lifestyle portfolio. Develop annual marketing calendar and actionable ATL+BTL plans with digital focus. Execute innovative campaigns from conceptualisation through creative and media agency briefs, to performance analysis. Custodian of brand operations ensuring seamless execution across all touchpoints.

## Core Responsibilities
- Lead brand equity campaigns for Myntra's fashion/lifestyle positioning.
- Develop and own annual marketing calendar and ATL+BTL plan.
- Brief and manage creative and media agencies.
- Execute multi-media campaigns (digital, TV, OOH, print).
- Analyse campaign performance and feed learnings back into strategy.
- Own brand operations and ensure consistency across touchpoints.

## Key Requirements
- 5-6 years in brand management and communication strategy
- Strong media planning (digital and traditional)
- Campaign creation, brand operations, creative development
- Comfort with multi-agency management
- Fashion/lifestyle or e-commerce background preferred
