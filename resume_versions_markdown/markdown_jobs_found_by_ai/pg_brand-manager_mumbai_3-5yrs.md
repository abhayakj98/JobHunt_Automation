# Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Procter & Gamble (P&G)
- Location: Mumbai
- Minimum experience: 3 years
- Status: Active

## Role Summary
Responsible for developing and executing brand strategies to drive consumer engagement and market share growth. The role involves managing cross-functional teams from Sales, Finance, Product Supply, Advertising, and Media Agencies. The Brand Manager ensures alignment of all brand activities with corporate strategy and oversees the brand's financial performance.

## Core Responsibilities
- Develop and execute integrated brand strategies to grow consumer engagement and market share.
- Own brand P&L and financial performance.
- Manage cross-functional teams (Sales, Finance, Product Supply, Advertising, Media Agencies).
- Ensure all brand activities align with corporate strategy.
- Lead media and communications planning for the brand.

## Key Requirements
- 3-5 years of experience in brand/marketing
- Bachelor's degree or MBA
- Proficiency in digital marketing and data analytics
- Strong cross-functional leadership
