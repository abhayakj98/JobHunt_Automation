# Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Marico Limited
- Location: Mumbai
- Minimum experience: 4 years
- Brands: Parachute, Saffola, Set Wet, Beardo, Nihar

## Role Summary
The Brand Manager owns the complete brand portfolio including strategy development, pricing, and profitability. Responsibilities include developing category business plans, analysing macro trends and consumer behaviour, managing product launches, overseeing advertising and media initiatives, and collaborating with sales and operations teams.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own brand strategy, pricing, and P&L.
- Develop annual category business plans from consumer and market insights.
- Manage product launches and product lifecycle decisions.
- Oversee advertising (ATL/BTL/Digital) and media planning.
- Collaborate with sales, finance, supply chain, and R&D.
- Analyse competitive landscape and consumer trends.

## Key Requirements
- 4-9 years in brand management/FMCG marketing
- MBA preferred
- Strong consumer insights, brand strategy, market analysis
- Communication and storytelling skills
