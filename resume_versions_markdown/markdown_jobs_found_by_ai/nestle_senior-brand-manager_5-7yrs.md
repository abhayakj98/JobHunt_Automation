# Senior Brand Manager

## Snapshot
- Company: Nestlé India
- Location: Mumbai / Gurgaon / Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 5 years
- Brands: Maggi, KitKat, Munch, Nescafé, Milkmaid, Milo

## Role Summary
Senior-level role providing strategic direction for brand portfolios and leading brand teams. The Senior Brand Manager develops long-term brand visions, manages substantial budgets, drives product innovation, oversees marketing strategy, and mentors junior staff. The role requires deep consumer understanding, market expertise, and strong business acumen.

## Core Responsibilities
- Develop long-term brand vision and annual brand plans.
- Own significant marketing budget with full P&L accountability.
- Drive product innovation pipeline from insight to launch.
- Lead ATL, BTL, digital, and media strategy.
- Collaborate with sales, finance, supply chain, and R&D.
- Mentor and develop junior brand team members.

## Key Requirements
- 5-7 years in brand management/marketing leadership
- MBA in Marketing or Business Administration required
- Strategic planning, brand building, innovation management
- Strong analytical skills and consumer understanding
- Team leadership experience
