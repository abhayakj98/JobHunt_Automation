# Brand Strategist

## Snapshot
- Company: Lenskart
- Location: Mumbai / Gurgaon
- Minimum experience: 4 years
- Scope: Eyewear lifestyle brand positioning and campaigns

## Role Summary
Owns brand development and strategy for Lenskart's eyewear products. Develops comprehensive brand positioning, manages brand campaigns across digital and traditional channels, creates content strategy aligned with market trends, and drives consumer awareness initiatives. Works with product and marketing teams to build strong brand equity in the competitive eyewear market.

## Core Responsibilities
- Define and own brand strategy and positioning for Lenskart.
- Build and manage brand campaigns across digital and traditional channels.
- Develop content strategy aligned with consumer trends.
- Drive consumer awareness and preference metrics.
- Collaborate with product, marketing, and creative teams.
- Manage agency relationships and execution quality.

## Key Requirements
- 4-8 years in brand/marketing
- Brand campaigns, content strategy, brand strategy, marketing communication
- Creative sensibility + analytical rigour
- Consumer insight-driven approach
