# Senior Manager – Brand Management

## Snapshot
- Company: Swiggy
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 3 years
- Scope: Swiggy private brands (food-tech, lifestyle)

## Role Summary
Brand owner for Swiggy's private brands, managing brand identity, positioning, and communication on and off-platform. Develops long-term growth strategies based on consumer insights and market trends. Drives brand awareness through 360-degree consumer initiatives. Responsible for brand metrics including user acquisition, retention, and repeat usage.

## Core Responsibilities
- Own brand identity, positioning, and communication strategy.
- Develop long-term brand growth plans from consumer and market insights.
- Build 360-degree consumer campaigns (on-platform and off-platform).
- Drive user acquisition, retention, and repeat usage metrics.
- Collaborate with product, marketing, and analytics teams.
- Manage agency relationships and campaign execution.

## Key Requirements
- 3-6 years in brand management
- MBA preferred (FMCG or FMCD background valued)
- Consumer behaviour analysis, P&L management
- Digital marketing, e-commerce, campaign development
