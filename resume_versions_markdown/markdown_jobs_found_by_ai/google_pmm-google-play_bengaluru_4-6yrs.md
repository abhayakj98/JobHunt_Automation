# Product Marketing Manager, Platforms and Devices (Google Play)

## Snapshot
- Company: Google
- Location: Bengaluru
- Minimum experience: 4 years
- Product context: Google Play — developer and consumer ecosystem

## Role Summary
Drive go-to-market strategy for Google Play in India. Partner with product and platform teams to launch features and communicate with developers and consumers. Develop growth marketing initiatives both in-app and off-app. Create messaging and positioning for Google Play services and execute regional campaigns for India-specific mobile ecosystem initiatives.

## Core Responsibilities
- Drive GTM strategy and execution for Google Play in India.
- Partner with product teams to launch features and communicate updates.
- Develop and execute growth marketing programmes (in-app + off-app).
- Build messaging and positioning for Google Play India.
- Use consumer data and research to inform strategy.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams and external agencies.

## Key Requirements
- 4+ years in marketing (growth, product marketing, brand, or social)
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience
- Consumer psychology and digital behaviour understanding
- Data-driven decision-making
- Cross-team project management
