# Resume Tailoring Learnings

## Core Principles

- Maintain a clean global base resume in Markdown and treat every application resume as a role-specific derivative.
- Consolidate Hindustan Unilever under one parent section to show employer continuity and total scope, then keep role changes distinct underneath.
- Make role headers metric-led. The first scan should communicate scope, business impact, and seniority.
- Prefer recent, job-relevant awards over low-signal academic or generic extracurricular detail.
- Keep Markdown heading hierarchy clean because it affects PDF conversion quality.

## Summary Rules

- The first sentence must explain the candidate in the language of the target role, not the previous role.
- Keep `data-oriented, insight-driven marketing professional` as a strong reusable anchor.
- Do not force brand-heavy summary language into growth, storefront, or analytics-led roles if it weakens fit.
- Once the Nestle full-time role is included, future resume iterations can position the candidate at `6+ years` for broad role matching; use role-specific judgment on whether `6 years` reads cleaner and remains more defensible for a specific application.
- The summary should lead with the hiring pattern for the role:
  - Brand roles: premium positioning, partnerships, culture, media, storytelling.
  - Product / growth / storefront roles: generating new trials, improving retention & increasing lifetime value, experimentation, customer engagement, conversion.

## Evidence Selection Rules

- Pull bullets upward when they directly mirror the JD keywords.
- De-emphasize backend analytics bullets unless they clearly connect to customer outcomes, experimentation, or business growth.
- Rephrase operational work into customer, conversion, merchandising, or decision-making impact where truthful.
- Mirror exact job language naturally when possible: for example `A/B testing`, `customer engagement`, `cross-functional program execution`, `data quality`, `storefront strategies`, `earned-owned-paid storytelling`.

## Role-Fit Critique Rules

- If a user-specified phrase harms role fit, do not apply it blindly. Note the conflict and optimize for a submit-worthy resume.
- For Amazon and similar growth roles, a Bacardi-style summary is directionally wrong if it over-indexes on premium positioning and experiential activations.
- For lifestyle or premium brand roles, a PMM-style summary can sound too SaaS-like unless it is balanced with brand-building proof.

## Quality Bar Before Submission

- Confirm all user non-negotiables are covered unless they directly conflict with role fit.
- Check for missing sections: contact block, summary, experience, skills, education, awards/leadership.
- Check for coherence issues such as conflicting seniority framing, duplicated headers, or mixed role narratives.
- Final resume should be skimmable in under 30 seconds and defensible in interview.

## Formatting And Layout Learnings

- Preserve the visual grammar of the approved global resume wherever possible: strong blue header, orange section rules, grey experience bars, and clear `CURRENT EXPERIENCE` / `PAST EXPERIENCE` structure.
- For experience sections, preserve original role titles and subtitles unless a rewrite materially improves candidacy. Prefer editing bullets before renaming headers.
- Bucket `Relevant Skills` into no more than 4 groups. This improves scanability without bloating the section.
- When a line or bullet exceeds one line by only 1-2 words, tighten only the obvious wrap-prone lines instead of broadly rewriting the resume.
- Bottom-page whitespace is useful. Use it to improve readability by increasing header emphasis and selective font sizing rather than forcing more content onto the page.
- Increase hierarchy carefully: section titles, date labels, role bars, and subheads can be enlarged slightly while keeping body copy stable and the resume on one page.

## Experience Structuring Learnings

- Keep HUL combined under one main employer section with distinct internal roles underneath.
- For Nestle R&D, merge internship and full-time experience into one company block when it strengthens continuity.
- The best Nestle format is:
  - company/role bar with the combined title,
  - one overall date range,
  - one subline for internship vs full-time dates,
  - one subline for the impact summary,
  - then shared bullets.

## Content Prioritization Learnings

- Add highly relevant awards when they strengthen role fit, for example `Best Experiential Marketing Activation in Homecare`.
- If a target role is storefront / growth oriented, emphasize customer engagement, experimentation, digital asset optimization, operating plans, and conversion before broader brand language.
- Tightening copy should preserve substance. Good edits usually remove low-signal connectors, repeated qualifiers, or long organization names rather than cutting proof points.
